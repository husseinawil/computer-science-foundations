1. Operational Complexity Summary
----------------------------------
* Stack Push:      O(1) Time | O(1) Space
* Stack Pop:       O(1) Time | O(1) Space
* Queue Enqueue:   O(1) Time | O(1) Space
* Queue Dequeue:   O(n) Time (using array shift) | O(1) Space
