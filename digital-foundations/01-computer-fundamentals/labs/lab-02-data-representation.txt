1. Number System Conversions
----------------------------
* Decimal:     42
* Binary:      00101010  (32 + 8 + 2)
* Hexadecimal: 0x2A

* Decimal:     255
* Binary:      11111111  (128 + 64 + 32 + 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 1)
* Hexadecimal: 0xFF

2. Text Encoding & Character Sets
---------------------------------
* ASCII 'A':   Decimal 65  | Binary 01000001 | Hex 0x41
* ASCII 'a':   Decimal 97  | Binary 01100001 | Hex 0x61
* ASCII '0':   Decimal 48  | Binary 00110000 | Hex 0x30

3. Bitwise Operations Verification
----------------------------------
* AND  (0x2A & 0x0F)  = 0x0A  (Masking lower nibble)
* OR   (0x20 | 0x0A)  = 0x2A  (Combining bitmasks)
* XOR  (0xFF ^ 0xAA)  = 0x55  (Inverting specific bits)
